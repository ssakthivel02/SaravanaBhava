About the App

Saravana Bhava – Murugan Bhakti is a Tamil devotional app created to help users read, learn, bookmark, and follow Hindu devotional content in a simple mobile-friendly format.

The app is Tamil-first and is being developed as a growing spiritual library with verified devotional content, meanings, and learning features.

Core Features

  • Tamil devotional songs and prayers
  • God-specific sections such as Murugan, Shiva, Perumal, Sakthi, Siddhars, and Vinayagar
  • Bookmarks for favourite content
  • Offline reading support where available
  • Quiz, profile, rewards, and achievement features
