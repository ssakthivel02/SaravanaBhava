About the App

Saravana Bhava – Murugan Bhakti helps users read Tamil devotional works, find reviewed and needs-review content, save bookmarks, use device Tamil text-to-speech and explore Murugan temples.

Content and temple records clearly display review status. Users should verify needs-review temple timings, addresses and travel information with official sources before visiting.

Core Features

  • Tamil devotional songs, kavachams, mantras and spiritual learning content
  • Saravana Bhava Guided Search across bundled local content
  • Bookmarks, reading streak, recently viewed content and local settings
  • Tamil device narration through text-to-speech; recorded audio only where available
  • Worldwide Murugan temple list with source and review status
  • Quiz, rewards, achievements and deity-specific sections

Privacy and Support

The app has no user account system and does not operate an advertising or analytics backend. Core preferences remain on the device. User-initiated maps, websites and approved remote media may open external services.
