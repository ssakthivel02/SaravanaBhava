Privacy Policy

App: Saravana Bhava – Murugan Bhakti
Last updated: 1 July 2026

1. Overview

Saravana Bhava – Murugan Bhakti is a devotional and spiritual learning app. This privacy policy explains how the app handles information.

2. Personal Data Collection

The app does not intentionally collect, sell, rent, or share personal information.

3. Local App Data

The app may store preferences, bookmarks, reading progress, quiz progress, profile name, and achievement status locally on the user's device to provide app functionality. This information remains on the device unless future cloud features are introduced and clearly disclosed.

4. Internet and External Links

The app may include links to external websites or media platforms in future versions. External websites have their own privacy policies, and users should review them separately.

5. Analytics, Ads, and Tracking

The app does not use advertising tracking, behavioural profiling, or third-party analytics in the current release.

6. Children

The app contains devotional and cultural content suitable for general audiences. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data Deletion

Since app data is stored locally, users can delete stored data by clearing the app storage or uninstalling the app. For support, contact the email below.

8. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when new features are added. The updated policy will be published on this page.
