Privacy Policy

App: Saravana Bhava – Murugan Bhakti
Package: com.saravanabhava.murugan
Current version: 1.1.0
Last updated: 28 July 2026

1. Overview

This policy explains how Saravana Bhava – Murugan Bhakti handles information. The app has no user-account system and does not operate an advertising, behavioural tracking or third-party analytics backend.

2. Local App Data

The app may store the following on the user's device to provide app functions:

  • bookmarks and recently viewed content
  • reading streak and font preference
  • notification preferences
  • quiz scores, reward points, achievements and optional local profile name

This information is not uploaded to a Saravana Bhava account or server. Android backup is disabled for the app build.

3. Internet, Audio, Text-to-Speech and External Links

Core devotional reading and guided search use bundled local content. Internet access may be used when a user chooses to open the official website, privacy/support pages, an external map, or an approved remote audio source. Tamil narration uses the device text-to-speech service. External services are governed by their own privacy policies.

4. Notifications

If enabled, daily reminders are scheduled locally on the device. The app requests notification permission where Android requires it and does not use a Saravana Bhava push-notification account to profile users.

5. Data Sharing

The current release does not sell, rent or share personal data. It does not include advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs.

6. Children

The app contains devotional and cultural content suitable for general audiences. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children and does not contain advertising or in-app purchases.

7. Data Deletion and Retention

Users can remove local data from the in-app Settings screen, clear the app's storage through Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Because there is no user account, there is no cloud account record to delete.

8. Content and Temple Sources

Some devotional or temple records are labelled “Needs Review”. This label concerns content verification and does not indicate personal-data collection. Users should verify travel and temple information with official sources.

9. Changes

Material changes will be published on this page with an updated date.
