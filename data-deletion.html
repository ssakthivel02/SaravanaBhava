Data Deletion Instructions

App: Saravana Bhava – Murugan Bhakti

Delete Local App Data

The current app stores user preferences, bookmarks, and progress locally on the device. To delete this data:

  1. Open Android Settings.
  2. Go to Apps.
  3. Select Saravana Bhava – Murugan Bhakti.
  4. Choose Storage.
  5. Tap Clear Data or uninstall the app.
