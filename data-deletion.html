Data Deletion Instructions

App: Saravana Bhava – Murugan Bhakti
Package: com.saravanabhava.murugan
Version: 1.1.0

The app has no user-account system. Bookmarks, reading progress, recently viewed items, preferences, quiz results, rewards and notification settings are stored locally on the device.

Option 1 — Delete inside the app

  1. Open Saravana Bhava.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Choose Delete All Data.
  4. Confirm and restart the app.

Option 2 — Clear Android app storage

  1. Open Android Settings.
  2. Choose Apps → Saravana Bhava – Murugan Bhakti.
  3. Choose Storage.
  4. Tap Clear Storage / Clear Data.

Option 3 — Uninstall

Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Android backup is disabled for this app build.
