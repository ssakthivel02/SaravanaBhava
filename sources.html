Sources and Review Status

Saravana Bhava contains bundled devotional, learning and Murugan temple records. Each important record should preserve its source or review status instead of presenting uncertain information as verified.

Source principles

  • Use recognised scripture editions, traditional devotional publications, official temple/trust information and other attributable references.
  • Keep Tamil text, transliteration, English meaning and commentary distinct.
  • Mark incomplete or unconfirmed records as Needs Review.
  • Verify temple timings, addresses, travel guidance and event schedules with the official temple or trust before visiting.
  • Record media provenance and usage rights before publishing audio or images.

Correction requests

Report the screen or record name, the disputed wording, and an authoritative reference through App Support.
