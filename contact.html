Before contacting support

  1. Restart the app.
  2. Check that your Android system and Tamil text-to-speech voice are updated.
  3. For temple timings, contact the official temple or trust because records labelled “Needs Review” may be incomplete.
  4. For local-data problems, use Settings → Delete All Data, then restart the app.

Report an issue

Include the screen name, button pressed, expected result, actual result, Android version, app version and a screenshot. Do not send passwords, payment details or private documents.
